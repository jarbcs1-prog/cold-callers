Two-minute demo script

Agent: "Welcome to cold-callers. This call is being recorded for compliance and training purposes. I'm evaluating whether this scenario is within our rule set. Understood — proceeding with the compliant talk track."
Agent (outbound): "Hi [lead], I'm [agent]. I'm calling about [offer] — does this remain compliant with your regulatory limits?"
Lead: "Yes, that's fine."
Agent: "Great. I'll now proceed with the approved talk track and log the entire call for your compliance record."

How it works

  1. Agent evaluates the call context (lead, script, rule set, user profile).
  2. Compliance gate validates the script, tone, and content against configured rules.
  3. Execute the call with full compliance logging and evidence tagging.
  4. Post-call capture: outcome, objections, next steps, and compliance record.
